## Executive verdict

Argus already has useful, tested primitives for immutable evidence, point-in-time candle selection, candidate and catalyst ledgers, TradePlan/ranking persistence, specialist opinions, rolling regime derivation, and non-transmitting broker simulation. Those primitives should be reused instead of replaced.

Argus does **not** yet have a reusable, general Strategy Science Lab or point-in-time replay kernel. The existing replay paths are narrow projections of stored evidence. They do not jointly reconstruct the eligible universe, feature state, scores, rank order, plans, rejection reasons, risk state, and event chronology under a replay-controlled clock. No reviewed historical session is currently proven to meet the directive's golden-day standard.

The historical-data foundation is also not broadly admissible. Locally inventoried datasets lack a durable security master, delisting and corporate-action lineage, declared price basis, and survivorship-controlled universe membership. Provider selection, contract/licensing review, and cost evaluation remain open. This agrees with the current Roadmap rather than expanding its claims.

## Classification standard

- `PROVEN_REUSABLE`: implemented, materially aligned to the lab need, and supported by focused passing tests or preserved evidence.
- `REUSABLE_WITH_EXTENSION`: a sound implementation exists, but the lab needs additional fields, adapters, time control, or broader evidence coverage.
- `NARROW_USE_CASE`: implemented and useful only for a bounded scenario; treating it as the general lab capability would overstate it.
- `UNPROVEN`: candidate evidence exists, but the reviewed repository does not prove it satisfies the proposed acceptance standard.
- `MISSING`: no material implementation or evidence was found.
- `CONFLICTING`: implementations or records make incompatible claims that must be resolved before reuse.

These labels describe fitness for the proposed Strategy Science Lab, not the quality of the existing feature in its original scope.

2. Presence on disk is not proof of a provider license, retention right, replay right, or redistribution right.
3. The lab may use only explicitly admitted, versioned source packets for bounded validation until the security-identity/price-basis and provider-contract gates are satisfied.

## Recommended architecture boundary

The first separately authorized implementation/proof slice should be `ARGUS-CURRENT-EDGE-RESEARCH-LEDGER-001`: an **offline, read-only contract and validation artifact** built from already preserved evidence. It should reuse the proven append-only candidate/catalyst ledgers, write-once capture/TradePlan packets, integrity hashes, and strategy/config/runtime identity fingerprints to prove this lifecycle:

`preserved immutable evidence -> frozen prediction packet with outcomes blank -> validation/non-mutation proof -> separately appended outcome record`

That slice may define and test eligible-universe, event-state, production candidate/rejection/decision, research feature/prediction, missing-data/coverage, model/prompt/code/config identity, and human-annotation fields against fixed local fixtures. It must not contact providers, schedule prospective collection, activate a sidecar in production, alter production decisions, fill outcomes early, or gain execution authority. Any runtime collection or activation requires a later, separately authorized task and its own proof gates.

After DATA-CORPACTION and the point-in-time data-contract/provider-admission prerequisites are satisfied, the next replay-orchestration slice should add `ReplayClock`, immutable `LabRunIdentity`, a point-in-time data gateway, an explicit dependency container, and deterministic execution-assumption identity around the existing strategy/ranking/TradePlan and FakeBroker-derived components:

`admitted/versioned source packets -> point-in-time gateway -> replay-controlled context -> existing strategy/ranking/TradePlan logic -> FakeBroker-derived execution model -> immutable run ledger/comparison report`

Candidate/catalyst ledgers, candle finality, ranking/TradePlan serialization, specialist opinion, rolling regime, and FakeBroker safety should be adapted rather than rewritten in either slice.
