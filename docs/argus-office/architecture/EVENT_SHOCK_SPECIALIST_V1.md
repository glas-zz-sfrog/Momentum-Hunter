# Event Shock Specialist v1

## Boundary

`momentum_hunter.event_shock_specialist` is a pure, deterministic research
layer over the existing CONTINUOUS-003 macro-event, catalyst-attribution,
market-regime, and common specialist-opinion contracts. It performs no I/O and
has no provider, account, broker, order, persistence, service, scheduler,
Engine Host, WPF, Paper, Shadow, or runtime activation capability.

Every common opinion is restricted to:

```text
authority = RESEARCH_ONLY
executionAuthority = EXECUTION_AUTHORITY_NONE
```

A headline, classification, hypothesis, confirmation, or later outcome cannot
admit a candidate, alter a score or TradePlan, size a position, veto a trade,
or create an order.

## Event And Relationship Identity

The versioned event taxonomy includes supply disruption, industrial incident,
geopolitical escalation, cyber incident, unexpected regulation, material
corporate event, and approved other shock. Existing catalyst relationships map
without reinterpretation to direct issuer, competitor, supplier/customer,
sector, macro, or unresolved. Commodity relevance requires a separate explicit
evidence reference; it is never inferred from a headline.

Unresolved, stale, malformed, future-dated, or insufficient evidence produces
an explicit abstention or failure. It cannot quietly become neutral evidence.
Canonical packet fingerprints bind the event, target opportunity/setup,
policy, evidence, prospective hypothesis, observed confirmation, and common
specialist opinion.

## Reaction Separation

The packet keeps three concepts separate:

1. Event relevance describes why the event may pertain to the target.
2. Expected reaction freezes the prospective directional or volatility
   hypothesis using only evidence available at classification time.
3. Market reaction measures completed canonical bars after classification.

Market reaction can confirm bullish, bearish, or non-directional volatility;
remain unconfirmed; show no material reaction; or fail for insufficient data.
Independent flags preserve news/price disagreement, volume without progress,
relative lag, and immediate breakout failure. The initial packet is immutable;
a later actual-reaction record attaches separately and cannot rewrite the
prospective hypothesis.

## Promotion Boundary

No producer, observer schedule, persistence path, specialist combination, or
strategy authority is part of v1. Later prospective collection must bind the
complete opportunity denominator supplied by STAT-DATA-002 and must preserve
the Momentum/Paper path as the control. Any influence on candidate admission,
ranking, TradePlan, risk, allocation, Paper, Shadow, or orders requires a new
prospective policy/sample identity and a separately authorized promotion task.
