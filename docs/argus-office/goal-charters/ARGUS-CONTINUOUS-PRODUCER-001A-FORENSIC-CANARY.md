# Goal Charter: ARGUS-CONTINUOUS-PRODUCER-001A-FORENSIC-CANARY

## User-Visible Goal

Prove with real Finviz and Schwab market data that the canonical Continuous
runtime naturally discovers, admits, prepares, reevaluates, and records a
truthful TradePlan or no-plan result, then reconstructs that state after an
isolated restart.

## Operator Pain

Producer-001A is canonical and synthetically proven, but a reviewer cannot yet
reconstruct a provider-backed natural decision from immutable physical evidence.
That blocks the prospective denominator and every later Continuous Paper gate.

## Scope

- Run the unchanged canonical Continuous discovery, readiness, composition,
  lifecycle, material-event, TradePlan, denominator, and restart classes.
- Contact only Finviz discovery and Schwab market-data/history endpoints.
- Use a new external write-once evidence root and disposable runtime stores.
- Preserve provider chronology, hot-universe admission, history admission and
  use, canonical bars, material reevaluation, lifecycle/setup records,
  TradePlan/no-plan records, restart continuity, and anti-hindsight evidence.
- Produce a manifest-rooted forensic packet and sanitized self-contained
  second-eye ZIP.

## Non-Goals

- No product-semantic repair, installed Continuous deployment, production-store
  write, scheduler/service change, opening mutation, RTD mutation, or strategy
  change.
- No account, balance, position, Paper, Shadow, preview, broker, order, or money
  movement capability.
- No candidate, setup, successor, material event, plan, or decision injection.
- No downstream STAT-DATA-002, instrument-classification, or Continuous Paper
  work before independent second-eye adjudication.

## Protected Areas

- Real provider access and Schwab OAuth state.
- Canonical and installed Continuous/opening runtime identity.
- Historical market evidence and production runtime stores.
- TradePlan chronology and every execution boundary.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The binding forensic standard exists at the approved path with SHA-256
   `8B3A7F161BA393DACCED20C92B6B544C3893D201A97F76B370980DA884940303`.
2. At least two real Finviz discovery cycles are preserved without manual
   ticker nomination; no-qualified-candidate remains a truthful bounded result.
3. Each natural candidate has reconstructable universe, Schwab history/current
   evidence, decision-use, completed-bar, material-event, lifecycle, and
   TradePlan/no-plan chronology where the market makes those stages legal.
4. A controlled isolated-runtime restart preserves state and identities without
   duplicates, rewrites, future evidence, or invented successors.
5. Unknown instrument execution remains blocked and all Paper/order authority
   remains unavailable.
6. Canonical Git, installed services/runtime, scheduler/manifest, opening
   evidence, production Continuous state, Paper, Shadow, RTD, and broker state
   remain unchanged.
7. The external packet is write-once, hash-manifested, secret-sanitized, and
   independently verified.
8. The second-eye ZIP contains the complete claimed call path, evidence,
   transitive local imports, tests, transcripts, and reruns focused verification
   without importing missing project-local modules.

## Required Evidence

- Exact source/tool/config/runtime identities and pre/post production baselines.
- Raw sanitized provider-derived records plus deterministic forensic timeline.
- Pre/post restart snapshots and duplicate/chronology adjudication.
- Focused, provider/backfill, opening-boundary, compile, PowerShell, secret,
  capability, protected-path, historical-nonmutation, and manifest checks.
- Immutable external packet and second-eye ZIP paths, counts, and SHA-256 values.

## Completion Rule

Done means the real bounded campaign is terminal, every required classification
is evidence-backed, the packet and ZIP independently verify, the task branch is
pushed, concise governance identifies the immutable evidence, production is
unchanged, and all downstream implementation remains stopped for second-eye
review.
