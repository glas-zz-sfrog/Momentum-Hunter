# Goal Charter: ARGUS-STRATEGY-SCIENCE-LAB-001

## Goal Statement

Produce a decision-ready, independently reviewed Strategy Science Lab review
packet that inventories Argus's actual reusable research and replay
infrastructure, defines point-in-time architecture and evidence contracts,
evaluates the required research methods against simpler baselines, designs the
validation and threat controls, and proposes a dependency-correct roadmap and
one exact next implementation directive without changing production behavior.

The packet must answer the directive's core question: using only information
genuinely available at the decision instant, can the simplest sufficient system
identify a small, high-value candidate tail whose probability-weighted,
executable payoff remains favorable after realistic costs, gap risk,
uncertainty, dependence, and the complete recorded research search process?

## User Pain / Operator Outcome

Steven receives one reviewable body of evidence that separates what Argus has
already proved from what is merely reusable, missing, conflicting, or still a
hypothesis. The packet must make it possible to approve, reject, or narrow the
program and its first implementation slice without granting a model, research
agent, replay result, or new subsystem any production or trading authority.

## Governing Design Invariants

1. Complexity must pay rent against an appropriate simpler baseline under the
   same period, point-in-time information, universe, execution, cost, and
   validation assumptions. If the simple model reproduces the decisions and
   economics, prefer it.
2. Prediction objects remain distinct from action utility. Event existence and
   timing, outcome probability, contemporaneous expectation/surprise, reaction
   distributions, tradeability, tail risk, uncertainty, and missingness must
   remain auditable rather than collapsing into a magical score.
3. Ranking, top-tail selection, explicit abstention, and zero-trade outcomes
   are first-class. No daily trade quota is permitted.
4. The exact frozen production Momentum strategy is the first replay baseline
   and must not be improved using replay outcomes.
5. Event classes remain distinct unless controlled partial pooling earns its
   use. Deterministic routing precedes learned mixture-of-experts designs.
6. LLMs begin as provenance-bound evidence workers. No LLM narrative may
   authorize a trade; present-day weights cannot silently become historical
   forecasting evidence.
7. Retraining, new features, challenger promotion, and threshold changes are
   offline, versioned, replayed, independently reviewed, and separately
   authorized. Nothing self-promotes.
8. Risk and capital assumptions remain percentage- or risk-unit-based rather
   than hardcoded to an account size.
9. Replay Clock is the sole simulated-time authority, and all historical
   strategy reads pass through a fail-closed Point-in-Time Data Gateway.
10. Outcomes append only after reveal and never mutate a frozen prediction
    packet: `PREDICT FIRST. FREEZE. REVEAL LATER.`
11. Recorded, derived, reconstructed, synthetic, unavailable, and inadmissible
    evidence remain explicitly distinguishable.
12. A theoretical edge that does not survive realistic executable assumptions
    is rejected.

## Protected Areas

This directive authorizes documentary inspection and design discussion of the
following protected areas, but authorizes no semantic or physical change:

- **Replay identity and historical capture selection:** inventory and define
  future fail-closed admission, chronology, source, code, strategy, model,
  configuration, feature, prompt, and simulation identities. Do not change
  replay logic or admit a historical event in code or data.
- **Core scoring, readiness, TradePlan, risk, and exits:** describe frozen
  baseline and future research boundaries only. Do not change current values,
  rules, identities, or behavior.
- **Database/schema/persistence:** propose logical ledgers, provenance fields,
  immutability, and append semantics only. Do not create or migrate a store.
- **Broker/provider/execution behavior:** inspect existing abstractions and
  design simulated execution/cost contracts only. Do not contact an account,
  enable a capability, or transmit, replace, or cancel an order.
- **Secrets, API keys, environment, production configuration, installed
  runtime, services, and schedules:** remain unread where not essential and
  byte-unchanged. No credential value belongs in the packet.
- **Current GUI and visual behavior:** unchanged; no UI implementation or
  visual-acceptance claim is in scope.
- **Canonical policy and Git integration:** the packet may propose a Roadmap,
  but cannot activate it. Non-fast-forward integration, rebase, reset, branch
  deletion, force-push, or unclear divergence requires Steven.
- **Licensing and paid services:** inventory terms, constraints, and estimated
  costs without accepting terms, creating an account, or committing spend.

Interrupt Steven if the work would mutate any protected semantics; reveal,
rotate, revoke, or delete a secret; touch another account; transmit a real
order; destroy user data; perform a schema migration; incur a paid commitment;
change production or installed behavior; or require a destructive/non-fast-
forward Git action. Also stop on an unexpected production-state, account,
permission, repository, evidence-integrity, or chronology anomaly.

  the sole simulated-time authority; routes strategy reads through a
  fail-closed Point-in-Time Data Gateway; delivers events in timestamp order;

  decision window, reveals evidence sequentially, runs the applicable frozen

- [ ] **A11 - Statistical validation.** The protocol requires chronological

  realistic effective sample size, DEVELOPMENT/VALIDATION/UNTOUCHED_HOLDOUT/
  PROSPECTIVE_SHADOW phases, complete experiment history, all preregistration
  fields, and multiple-testing/selection-bias controls.
- [ ] **A12 - Metrics, controls, and robustness.** The plan includes every
  Section XV metric, every Section XVI negative control/placebo, and every
  Section XVII stress. It analyzes results with and without largest winners
  without discarding legitimate asymmetry and rejects idealized-only edge.
- [ ] **A13 - Agentic challenger.** The present-day challenger is designed only
  as `SHADOW_ONLY`, starts with one strong agent, deterministic tools, and one

  agent swarm.
- [ ] **A14 - Preregistered research families.** H1 earnings expectation
  divergence, H2 relationship propagation, H3 scout plus confirmation, H4

  No hypothesis may be edited after outcomes become visible.
- [ ] **A15 - Dependency-aware roadmap.** The proposal addresses all 19
  Section XXI sequence entries (0 through 18), preserves existing accepted

  does not claim Roadmap placement is activation.
- [ ] **A16 - Exact next directive.** The packet ends with one bounded,
  implementation-ready proposed directive, including authority, prerequisites,

  `ARGUS-CURRENT-EDGE-RESEARCH-LEDGER-001` first slice or requires a narrower

- [ ] **A17 - Freeze and authority proof.** Final diff and repository evidence
  show documentation-only changes. Production code, tests, packages, schemas,
  configs, data, runtime, services, providers, brokers, orders, Paper, Shadow,
  GUI, installed artifacts, and canonical policy behavior remain unchanged.
