# ARGUS-CONTINUOUS-PRODUCER-001D-FORENSIC-CANARY

## Goal

Run one bounded real-provider, research-only canary against the exact Producer-001D product commit after the offline exact-path proof passes.

## Boundaries

- The wrapper is observational only; canonical production classes own discovery, hot-universe admission, readiness, completed-bar dispatch, natural composition, and producer persistence.
- Finviz and Schwab market-data/history are the only provider paths.
- Account values, positions, Paper, Shadow, broker operations, and orders remain unavailable.
- Failed Producer-001A, Producer-001B, and Producer-001C evidence remains immutable and hash-bound.
- Any terminal outcome is preserved, sealed, sanitized, packaged, extracted, and stopped for independent second-eye review.

## Acceptance

- Product source is the clean detached commit named by the wrapper.
- Production canonical remains clean and unchanged.
- The offline exact-path replay is hash-bound and passed before provider contact.
- Completed-bar event records preserve provider and receipt clocks.
- Stage accounting preserves upstream success when a later stage fails.
- The terminal packet passes sanitation and manifest verification; failures remain failures.
