# Goal Charter: ARGUS-CONTINUOUS-TRADEPLAN-PRODUCER-001A

## User-Visible Goal

The installed Continuous research architecture can naturally turn recurring
discovery and newly completed canonical bars into immutable lifecycle, setup,
and DATA-004 TradePlan evidence without test or operator injection.

## Operator Pain

The lower-level producer is valid, but the natural runtime currently supplies
no lifecycle or successor evidence and emits no recurring completed-bar event.
It can therefore discover and prepare a symbol without naturally reaching the
TradePlan behavior already proven through synthetic direct calls.

## Scope

- Reuse the existing sequential-breakout research detector, candidate lifecycle,
  Continuous composition, canonical candle, RVOL, and DATA-004 contracts.
- Own prospective opportunity lifecycle and setup chronology under the runtime
  state root.
- Dispatch material events only for newly observed completed canonical bars and
  preserve the exact changed-bar identity.
- Reevaluate the existing producer for natural continuation, missed-entry,
  pullback, reclaim, invalidation, and predecessor-linked successor evidence.
- Deterministically reconstruct universe, lifecycle, setup, material-event, and
  existing-plan state after restart.
- Preserve a research-visible plan for unknown instrument classification while
  retaining an explicit execution blocker.

## Non-Goals

- No provider-backed canary, BMNR replay, catalyst memory, multi-resolution
  policy, new setup vocabulary, or new provider.
- No opening-strategy, scoring, ranking, Paper, Shadow, account, position,
  broker, order, WPF, or live-execution behavior.
- No inference of instrument subtype from ticker or name.

## Protected Areas

- Continuous runtime orchestration and readiness.
- Candidate lifecycle, setup, predecessor, and TradePlan identity.
- Canonical candle/history persistence and evidence chronology.
- Installed opening runtime and all execution boundaries.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Natural composition creates lifecycle state for a newly admitted member.
2. Completed canonical bars, not five-second polling ticks or provisional bars,
   dispatch bounded material reevaluation with exact source identity.
3. Deterministic bars naturally produce a missed entry followed by a distinct
   successor setup and predecessor-linked TradePlan without caller injection.
4. Adequate backfilled history plus one current completed bar remains sufficient
   for readiness; there is no five-new-bar ceremony.
5. Missing, stale, future-known, conflicting, or tampered evidence fails closed.
6. Restart preserves missed-entry truth, setup/predecessor identity, processed
   material events, and plan identities without duplicates or hindsight.
7. Unknown instrument classification leaves a research plan visible but marks
   it execution-ineligible with `INSTRUMENT_CLASSIFICATION_UNAVAILABLE`.
8. Existing opening evidence is byte-identical and the approved opening runtime,
   services, scheduler, Paper/Shadow state, and order capability are unchanged.
9. Focused, adjacent, full Python, compile, boundary, protected-path, secret,
   and nonmutation verification passes.

## Required Evidence

- Focused natural-path tests covering lifecycle ownership, canonical-bar event
  dispatch, missed-entry successor chronology, restart, arbitrary same-session
  startup, unknown instrument blocking, anti-hindsight, and idempotency.
- Broader Continuous, candle, RVOL, lifecycle, TradePlan, and opening regressions.
- Opening dependency-closure impact and installed-state nonmutation proof.
- A later separately authorized provider-backed forensic canary under the
  externally frozen Producer-001A evidence standard.

## Completion Rule

Done means the natural path is implemented and proven on the task branch, Hard
Chew is green, governance records exact evidence and Git state, no production
runtime was changed, and the result is ready only for the separate provider-
backed research canary.
